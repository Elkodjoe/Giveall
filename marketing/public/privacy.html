Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

GiveAll ("we," "the app") is a relationship-fitness app. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. We wrote it to match exactly what the app actually does — not boilerplate.

No account or email required to use the app

GiveAll signs you in anonymously (Firebase Anonymous Authentication) the first time you open it. This creates a random identifier tied to your device install — we never ask for your name, email, or phone number to use the app itself.

What we store, and why

Everything below is stored in Firestore (Google Cloud infrastructure) under your anonymous identifier, used only to run the app's core features:

  • Daily check-ins — your self-reported Attractive/Valued/Wanted scores and mood, so the app can prescribe a relevant daily action.
  • Bid Tracker entries — the small moments of connection you choose to log.
  • Memory Vault — specific details you save about a partner, used to personalize prescribed actions. You can delete your entire Memory Vault at any time from Settings.
  • Desire Inventory — what you say makes you (or your partner) feel wanted.
  • Curiosity Card progress and onboarding answers — your attachment style and love language, derived from your onboarding answers, used to tailor prescriptions.
  • Notification preferences — your chosen check-in time. Reminders are scheduled entirely on your device (Expo local notifications) — we don't operate a push server and no notification token ever leaves your device.

Partner Mode is opt-in, and requires both people to agree

Partner Mode is off by default. If you turn it on and share a connection code, your partner only gains read access to your profile after both of you explicitly opt in — enforced by our database security rules, not just app-side logic. Neither person can unilaterally grant themselves access to the other's data.

The one place we use a third-party AI provider

GiveAll's "Appreciation Generator" can turn a generic compliment into a more specific, personal line. When this feature calls a cloud AI provider (Anthropic or OpenAI) rather than a model running locally, it sends only what you typed — the generic compliment, your love language, and optionally a Memory Vault detail you chose to include — to generate the response. That text is not stored by us beyond the request, and is subject to the AI provider's own data-handling terms for API requests.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data, to anyone, for any reason.
  • We don't run ads, and we don't use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • We don't build an advertising profile of you or share your data with data brokers.

The waitlist / early-access signup

If you sign up for early access on our landing page, we store only the email address you provide and the time you signed up, so we can notify you when access opens. This list is not readable through the app or any public interface — it's write-only from the signup form.

Deleting your data

You can delete your Memory Vault at any time from Settings. To request deletion of all data tied to your anonymous identifier, or to ask us anything about this policy, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

GiveAll is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where required, notify you in-app.
